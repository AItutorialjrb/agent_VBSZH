## 1. Conventions
- σ_gen = MG5 cross section of the generated sample (MC error from integration). σ_phys = σ_gen × external BR (B_γγ = 0.002270 for forced-H samples; ×0.9783 PDG/MG rescale where Z→ℓℓ is in the ME). The "σ_phys×L" column is the expected number of data events in the generated phase space at 500 fb⁻¹.
- Raw event weight = σ_phys·L / N_planned. Requirement: N ≥ max(50k, σ_phys·L), i.e. weight ≤ 1. For fake-photon samples the physical per-event weight is the raw weight × the fake factor; that weighted value is required to be ≤ 1 in the VBS region and the peak window (§3).
- SR (for MC-stat adequacy only) = illustrative VBS selection (m_jj>500, |Δη_jj|>2.5) + 120<m_γγ<130 GeV. It is **not optimised**. Its expected total is 2.67 events (data-stat ±1.63), dominated by labelled estimates.
- ESTIMATE rows are not measurements; each is replaced by the gridpack-job σ before production.

## 4. MC-statistics adequacy (SR, 500 fb⁻¹)
Target: MC-stat uncertainty / data-stat uncertainty ≤ 0.3 per sample, plus ≥ 10 effective MC events in the SR so that no component is reported as a nominal zero. Data-stat in the SR is ±1.63 events (expected total 2.67). Every sample with an estimated SR efficiency reaches a ratio ≤ 0.024 (largest: F_QCD_lla_mlm). Samples without an SR efficiency estimate (offshell systematic, I_ZHjj, tHW, ZH→ττ, I_llaajj, tops, multiboson) have SR yields ≤ 0.05 events. Even with a 100 % MC-stat error, their ratio stays ≤ 0.03. The binding constraints are therefore the 50k floor, weight ≤ 1, and the N_eff ≥ 10 rule in the PEAK window (Z+jets: 810 ✓; Zγ+jets: ~226 est. ✓; all others ≥ 48).

**The 8-core 3-day llγγ jobs are replaced.** Interactively, the EW llγγjj survey completed 0/1057 channels and QCD llγγ+0–2j 1/448 channels in 25 min. That implies ≥16 core-min per channel, so a single EW gridpack needs ≥ 560 core-h, i.e. ≥ 70 h on 8 cores, which risks the 72 h testmatch limit with no margin. New layout:
- QCD llγγ split by **jet multiplicity** into 0j (1 core), 1j (8 cores, workday) and 2j. MLM combines separately generated multiplicities, with Pythia nJetMax = 2 for all.
- The 2j part and EW llγγjj are each split into **4 disjoint beam-1 parton classes** (≈ ¼ of the channels each, ≈ 18 h on 8 cores, run in parallel).
- Split closure on Z+2j is −1.4 % (pull −2.1). It must be re-checked at higher precision before relying on the split.
- I_llaajj (signed EW×QCD continuum; reference estimate ≈ 0.0085 fb with the m_γγ cut, 6 % of EW llγγjj) is as heavy as both together. **Recommendation: defer its event sample** and use σ only (D-P3).
