The nominal filter loses 25/29 % at the VBS stage (04:149,154), and production switches to the looser twin filter whose residual loss is "assumed, not measured" (04:13,115-116). That violates SPEC §4.3(1) ("proven safe with twin samples"). The bound "residual ≤ observed loss" also has no basis. At the two-fat-jet stage ihtmin 500 already loses 43-46 % (04:147,152), even though reco needs ΣpT(fat jets) ≥ 750 GeV. The VBS filter 3.0/400 loses 25 % against reco 3.5/500 (tagging_scenarios.yaml:25). So the SR multijet is largely populated by shower jets, the parton-HT/mjj distribution of SR events extends well below the cut, and the 350 / 2.0-200 cut can lose a comparable fraction. Condition: hold the QCD block (185 jobs, 04:407 item 3) until D-HH-14(a) is answered with a measurement. Options: a further-loosened twin (~1e6 events ≈ 40 wall-h, cheaper than the 68 h block), or a Pythia/particle-level fat-jet filter that is safe by construction.
