# Review hh Phases 01-03 (gate G1), multi_r1, reviewer, 2026-11-03 UTC
Paths relative to p2/runs/multi_r1/hh/. Read-only review. Exit codes, N, sigma/BR closure and seeds are taken from the verifier and not re-audited.

## Severity 1 (changes a result or violates handoff §1)
1. The truth-tag implementation contradicts the pre-declared scenarios. scripts/sr_feasibility.py:36,40 maps every fat jet with nB>=2 (g->bb, Z->bb as well as H->bb) to eff_Hbb_contained (0.60 nominal). It maps nB==1 to mistag_g2bb_or_b. reports/02_background_overlap.md:69-75 and config/tagging_scenarios.yaml:8-10 say g->bb gets the 5 % rate. The per-jet weight on PS g->bb jets in QCD/tt is therefore ambiguous by up to 12x, and so is every number in 02 §8 (lines 83-96). The scenarios have to be unambiguous before Phase 05 (handoff §5.2 "pre-declared"). Declare an explicit bb-non-H class (g->bb and Z->bb, chosen deliberately) and re-run 02 §8.

## Severity 2 (must fix before G2)
2. The ME jet multiplicity is below what the SR needs for several backgrounds, and this is not raised as a decision. The SR has 2 fat jets + 2 VBS jets. V_Zjj_QCD_vbs (`z j j`, 02:42) and H_VBFH_bb (`h j j`, 02:36) need one PS jet in the SR, H_ZH/WH (`z h`, 02:37-39) need both VBS jets from the PS, and ggH+jj needs one more. Only ggF HH (D-HH-2) and tt (D-HH-3) are flagged. Add a decision for Z/W+3j and VH+2j (or matched samples), or quote a PS-modelling uncertainty.
3. Morphing closure is narrow. Both closure points have kV=kl=1 (01:72,84-85), so no closure tests the kV^3kl, kV^2kl^2 or kV k2V kl monomials away from the basis. The closure is also inclusive-parton-level with 5k events (01:89, m(HH) 250-2250). It is not done in the boosted SR, where SM acceptance is about 0.1 % and the basis cross sections span 1.15-119 fb (cancellations). Needed at G2: one closure point with kV!=1 and kl!=1, Delphes-level SR closure, and Sum|c_i| / MC-stat amplification over the intended k2V and kl scan ranges. Also, 01:90 cites D-HH-4, which is the QCD decision, not morphing statistics.
4. The effective-N policy is used where brute force is cheap. ggF HH (brute-force N 1.5e4, 02:27-29), ZH (1.0e5), ZZjj (1.1e5) and ttH (4.3e5) are labelled "effective-N". The §5.3 justification is that brute force is impossible, which is true only for QCD/tt/Z+jets. Apply max(50k, σ·BR·3000 fb⁻¹) to these samples, or log the choice as a deviation.
5. The upper-bound loophole in nmc_check: scripts/nmc_check_hh.py:30 accepts any non-None ul_fb. It does not check how the bound was derived (for example a Poisson 95 % bound with the weight scale) or that the conservative variant is actually produced. This is the ZH tt̄γ failure mode the handoff warns about.
6. The QCD/tt N_eff plan is naive. The "10^5-10^6 events" estimate (02:96) scales raw counts. With truth-tag weights that span 1e-4 (light,light) to 0.36 (bb,bb), N_eff is set by the rare PS g->bb fraction, which depends on issue 1. Give a class-resolved estimate, or a b-enriched/PS-bb strategy for Q_4j.
7. Phase 03 "one test job per sample type" is not met for the handoff-mandated types W±H, ggH+jj, W+jets, QCD 4b and tt̄jj. All are still "planned, no gridpack" (02:33,35,38-39,41,43). Despite this, INDEX.md:6 says "PASS (all 10 types)" and 03:4 still says Q_4j is IN-PROGRESS (stale). Correct the status lines and attach test jobs before G2.
8. Generator-filter safety is shown only at parton level and only for the signal (01:51, inclusive VBS phase space, not the boosted tail). The background twins (ihtmin, VBS 3.0/400, ptheavy) do not exist (02:78, 03:78). Required before G2, as acknowledged.
9. Deviations are missing from SPEC §7 (SPEC.md:107+): (a) the W factor is not derived (01:98 calls it a "stated deviation"), (b) the frozen MG5 install was modified (partial HEPTools install, D-HH-1, STATUS:13), (c) diagram audits ran in a private MG5 copy, work/mg5priv (work/diag/new.log), whose checksum is not recorded.

## Severity 3 (minor)
10. 01:62(c) says the ggF HH overlap is empty and that ggF HH "cannot populate the VBS-jet region". 02:85 shows ggF HH (LO, no K-factor) at about 50 % of the SM signal in the anchor SR. Reword the sentence; the conclusion of D-HH-2 is unaffected.
11. The "SM ~0.07 events" figure (02:91,96; STATUS:17) rests on 2 raw events. Quote a Poisson interval: the a-priori-negligible SM sensitivity conclusion holds even at the upper end, so the κ2V≠1 framing is supported and is not an overclaim. Show 0-count rows in 02 §8 as 95 % bounds, not "0 / 0 / 0".
12. Some backgrounds are neither in the ledger nor bounded: tW (all-hadronic, boosted), ttZ/ttW, QCD ZZ/WZ, gg->ZH (boosted), EW Zjj/Wjj. List each with a bound or a stated reason.
13. Trigger: ε=1 at leading AK8 pT 450 (tagging_scenarios.yaml trigger) is likely below the Run-3 single-AK8 plateau. Pre-declare a 500/550 variation now. Fat-jet m_SD has no JES (peak at 116 GeV, 03:65); the window choice in Phase 06 must account for this.
14. Decision lists are inconsistent: the 01:4 and 03:91 headers cite D-HH-1..5, while STATUS:18 has D-HH-6, which is missing from 02 §9. Missing decisions: the m(HH) clipping value (needed "with and without" by handoff §5.2), the tagging-class definition (issue 1), the jet-multiplicity samples (issue 2), and the trigger threshold.

Gauge invariance and the VH-like part are handled correctly: QCD=0/QED=4 is the full tree amplitude (QCD=1,2 gives no diagrams; 01:33-36), the s-channel labels are bookkeeping only, and the VHH resonant region is removed by mjj. The kappa-restriction bugs are cleared: all reported signal numbers trace to gridpacks_v2 (lhe_closure.sub, test_hh_A.sub, make_matrix.py). The six-monomial basis is correct (scripts/morph.py:8) and well conditioned (cond 8.66).

Verdict: PASS-with-conditions (fix 1 and restate 02 §8; 2-9 before G2)
