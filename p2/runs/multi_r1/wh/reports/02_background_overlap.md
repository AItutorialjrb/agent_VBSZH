## 1. Final state and measurement definition
Reco target: one isolated e/μ (W→ℓν), MET, ≥2 isolated photons with m_γγ in the Higgs window (sidebands reserved, Phase 06), ≥2 VBS tag jets, b-veto (top). **Signal = EW (0,4) W±Hjj (light external flavours, W→e/μ in ME, plus the τ feed-down as a separate signal-like component).** Everything with a resonant m_γγ peak that is not EW W±Hjj is a *resonant background* and is fitted with the signal shape; continuum (prompt γγ, fake γ) is constrained by m_γγ sidebands.

**Generator-cut handling (G1 item 2, D8; G2 item 2).** *Nominal for the frozen Zγγjj QCD lost-lepton component is now the frozen no-mjj twin itself* (`ZH_Zaajj_QCD_nomjj_lostlep`, ZH `B_QCD_zaajj_nomjj`, 100 000 events, σ = 0.273 fb, N_required 50 000, 280 events at the SR proxy; no C needed); the mmjj>400 sample is kept only as the C cross-check. For `ZH_Zajj_eFake` the full no-mjj N would be 1.05·σ·k·L = 2.57×10⁶ events but only 200 000 no-mjj events exist, so the QCD part keeps vbs×C (deviation, below). The frozen VBS-biased QCD-type samples (Zγγjj, Zγjj) were generated with mmjj>400, which is not safe for QCD-type processes (W twins at the loose selection: 47.6 ± 5.5 % / 37 ± 9 %). Following D8 the ZH twin-correction method is applied to the frozen samples (a **deviation**: nominal would be no-mjj samples; regeneration of Z lost-lepton samples was not planned): C = [σ·n_sel/N]_nomjj / [σ·n_sel/N]_mmjj>400 measured with the **WH reco-SR proxy** (`scripts/srsel.py`: exactly 1 lepton pT>25, MET>30, b-veto, photons, ≥2 jets with m_jj>500 and |Δη|>2.5) on the frozen ZH twin pairs (read-only): `ZH_Zaajj_QCD_lostlep` (B_QCD_zaajj nomjj/vbs, 100k + 100k events, ng=2): **C = 1.95 ± 0.13 (stat)**, i.e. 48.8 % of the no-cut yield lost (`work/ratioC_ZH_Zaajj_QCD.json`); QCD part of `ZH_Zajj_eFake` (F_QCD_llajj nomjj/vbs, 200k + 200k events, ng=1): **C = 1.87 ± 0.09 (stat)**, 46.4 % lost (`work/ratioC_ZH_Zajj_QCD.json`). Uncertainty on C = stat ⊕ full difference (C − 1) as a conservative systematic (ZH convention). The EW part (`ZH_Zaajj_EW_lostlep`, EW part of `ZH_Zajj_eFake`) has no frozen no-mjj twin: the W EW twin (`B_EW_Waajj_nomjj` vs `B_EW_Waajj_vbs`, `F_EW_Wajj_nomjj` vs `F_EW_Wajj_vbs`) is used as a proxy with the full difference as a systematic (PENDING gridpacks). Own W samples need no correction: nominal samples have no mmjj cut. Generator cuts that remain and are **not** twin-tested: ptj>20, pta>25, 100<m_γγ<170 (reco window 120-130 inside; photon pT>25 offline has no margin below the generator cut: migration of photons with true pT<25 to reco >25 is a small effect measured in Phase 04, as ZH), |η_j|<5. C is applied in Phase 05; no yield is quoted here.

## 4. Overlap-removal contract (hard/soft photons), generator-cut safety
Same partition-of-phase-space rule as ZH (02 §3), with the GenTruth `n_prompt_photons` (status-1 photon with no hadron in its ancestry, pT>15, |η|<2.5, ΔR>0.4 to prompt leptons pT>10 / other prompt photons) produced in-job by the reused `slim_gentruth.py`:
1. W+jets-type samples (F_Wjj) keep N_prompt = 0; Wγ(+jets) keeps N_prompt ≤ 1 (≥2 → covered by B_*Waajj); Wγγ keeps N_prompt ≥ 2. Residual migration in 15<pT<25 GeV (the B samples are generated with pta>25) is measured on the twins in Phase 04 (as ZH).
2. Shower QED emission on in all samples (PY8 default). Fake-photon pT = x·pT(jet), x~N(0.85,0.10) (unchanged from ZH).
3. tt̄γ/tWγ vs tt̄γγ (G1 item 4, D10): **events with ≥2 prompt photons are removed from `T_tta_1l` and `T_tWa_1l`** and the tt̄γγ ME sample `T_ttaa_1l` (σ·k = 0.554 fb ≈ 19× the signal σ_phys before selection) is kept in full, as in the ZH closeout. The removed yield is evaluated at reco level after b-veto from the test jobs in `04_production_plan.md` §6 (no "negligible" claim); if that evaluation shows the removed piece is not covered by T_ttaa_1l (second photon from top-decay products), the residual is carried as a 100 % systematic on T_tta_1l.
4. EW vs QCD by coupling order (diagram counts Phase 01 §4); interference separate and signed.
5. Generator-level cut safety by **twin samples** (SUPERSEDED by D8 and the revised paragraph in §3; this item kept for history) (as ZH A1): `B_QCD_Waajj_nomjj`/`F_QCD_Wajj_nomjj` (mmjj=0) vs the `*_vbs` samples (mmjj=400); safety = fraction of reco-selected (m_jj>500, Δη>2.5 + γγ + lepton) twin events whose generator m_jj < 400.
