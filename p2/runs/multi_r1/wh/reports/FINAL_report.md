B 860 (2025) 139202, arXiv:2405.16566 (138 fb^-1, 13 TeV, WH -> l nu bb via VBS): SM analysis observed (expected) 95 % CL upper limit 14.3 (9.9) on the VBS WH cross section ratio, signal significance 0.34 (0.22) sigma (text before the BSM section; abstract); lambda_WZ < 0 excluded beyond 5 sigma, consistent with the SM (abstract; Fig. 5).
* ATLAS, Phys. Rev. Lett. 133 (2024) 141801, arXiv:2402.00426 (140 fb^-1, 13 TeV, VBF WH -> l nu bb): observed (expected) upper limit 9.0 (8.7) times the SM VBF WH cross section; opposite-sign coupling hypothesis excluded beyond 5 sigma (abstract; Fig. 2, Table 2 for the SR yields).
* This study (H -> gamma gamma channel, 13.6 TeV, LO Delphes): the exact-Poisson counting expected mu95 at 140 fb^-1 is 17.5 (the PL asymptotic value 17.2 is less reliable at b about 0.5 events), against 14.3 (9.9) and 9.0 (8.7) of the published bb channels. Differences that matter for any comparison: H -> bb vs gamma gamma branching ratio (58 % vs 0.23 %) with very different backgrounds, sqrt(s) 13 vs 13.6 TeV, the signal definition (CMS: SM VBS WH; ATLAS: VBF WH; here EW WHjj, definition (a)/(b)), LO generation and a parametrised Delphes detector without pile-up. For lambda_WZ at 140 fb^-1 the DIRECT VH fits give lambda=-1 at 2.01 sigma (kZ-sign, convention-independent) or 3.35 sigma (kW-sign convention, D46), and 0.21 sigma for the whole lambda<0 region; this is consistent with, and weaker than, the >5 sigma exclusions of the bb channels (the headline of this study is not in tension with them: it is a different, much less sensitive channel).
