B 860 (2025) 139202, arXiv:2405.16566 (138 fb^-1, 13 TeV, WH -> l nu bb via VBS): SM analysis observed (expected) 95 % CL upper limit 14.3 (9.9) on the VBS WH cross section ratio, signal significance 0.34 (0.22) sigma (text before the BSM section; abstract); lambda_WZ < 0 excluded beyond 5 sigma, consistent with the SM (abstract; Fig. 5).

* This study (H -> gamma gamma channel, 13.6 TeV, LO Delphes): the exact-Poisson counting expected mu95 at 140 fb^-1 is 17.5 (the PL asymptotic value 17.3 is less reliable at b about 0.5 events), against 14.3 (9.9) and 9.0 (8.7) of the published bb channels. Differences that matter for any comparison: H -> bb vs gamma gamma branching ratio (58 % vs 0.23 %) with very different backgrounds, sqrt(s) 13 vs 13.6 TeV, the signal definition (CMS: SM VBS WH; ATLAS: VBF WH; here EW WHjj, definition (a)/(b)), LO generation and a parametrised Delphes detector without pile-up. For lambda_WZ at 140 fb^-1 the sqrt(L) scaling of the 500 fb^-1 result gives about 2.0 sigma (convention-independent, lambda = -1) or 3.3 sigma (kW-sign convention), and below 1 sigma for the whole lambda<0 region; this is consistent with, and weaker than, the >5 sigma exclusions of the bb channels (the headline of this study is not in tension with them: it is a different, much less sensitive channel).
