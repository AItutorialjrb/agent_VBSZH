## 7b. σ closure tolerance and the 1940/2000 case (VERIFY #6)
**Tolerance:** for 2000-event tests the PY8 σ (Σ of the LHE weights seen by Pythia) must agree with the gridpack/LHE-init σ within **±2 %**; for ≥ 8000-event jobs within **±1 %**. Rationale: the PY8 estimate is *not* an independent measurement. A MadGraph gridpack distributes the requested events over its ~50 integration channels in proportion to the survey σ_channel with integer rounding and per-channel maximum-weight truncation, so the channel mix of one small sample fluctuates around the true mix by O(1/√N_channel-events), independent of the quoted σ error (the quoted ± ignores this, hence z = 4-11 for 0.6-1.6 %). Evidence that it is a small-N effect and not a bias: the 5 × 8000-event twin jobs give per-job (PY8/gridpack − 1) of −0.5 ± 0.3 % (B_QCD_nomjj), −0.07 ± 0.15 % (F_QCD_nomjj, whose 2000-event test gave +0.9 %, z = 4), −0.3 ± 0.2 % (F_EW_nomjj; per-job sd 0.15-0.3 %, means consistent with 0). The z > 3 cases at 2000 events: V_WWa_WH +1.6 %, F_Wjjjj −1.2 %, V_WZa_WH −0.6 %, H_QCD_WHjj_fixed +1.0 % are all inside ±2 %; they are not corrected, the analysis weight uses the gridpack σ and the total event count (not the per-job PY8 σ), and the **production closure criterion** (Phase 04) is the sample-aggregate PY8/gridpack ratio within ±1 % over all jobs. N is sized from max(gridpack σ, PY8 σ) × 1.05, which covers ±2 % closure plus the 1940/2000 shortfall.
**Q_aajj** (5000-event test): gridpack σ vs PY8 +1.2 % (2.1σ) inside the ±2 % tolerance. **I_WHjj closure (not covered by the ±2 % rule):** gridpack σ = −0.2503 ± 0.0042 fb vs test PY8 σ = −0.2361 ± 0.0536 fb (−5.7 %). The sample has signed weights (45 % positive events at the parton level of the 2000-event test), so the PY8 estimate has a 23 % statistical error: the two agree at 0.3σ. The ±2 % tolerance applies to unweighted samples only. I_WHjj is σ-only (production dropped, G2 item 8); the gridpack σ is used.
**B_EW_Waajj_vbs test (885078): n_lhe = 1940 of 2000 requested**, no MLM.
