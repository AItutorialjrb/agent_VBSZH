**Lesson for production:** MG5 integration errors at 500–1000-event targets understated the true spread by about 3× in this process family. Production σ must come from the inverse-variance combination of many jobs' LHE `<init>` values (as in ZH `tier1_sigma`), or from a dedicated high-precision run, never from one low-target integration.
