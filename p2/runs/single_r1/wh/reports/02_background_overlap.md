## 3. Overlap-removal contract (phase-space partitions, not ancestry labels)
1. **Prompt photons** are defined once, in `lib/scripts/slim_gentruth.py` (inherited from ZH): status-1 photons with no hadron ancestor, p_T > 15, |η| < 2.5, isolated (ΔR > 0.4) from prompt leptons, other such photons and gen jets → `n_prompt_photons` (N_pγ). Per family: W+jets keeps N_pγ = 0, Wγ+jets N_pγ = 1, Wγγ+jets N_pγ ≥ 2; the same for tt̄ / tt̄γ / tt̄γγ, tWγ (=1), Zγjj (=1) and Zγγjj (≥ 2). The QED shower stays on in all samples (`py8_common_CP5.cmnd`). The generator thresholds (p_T^γ > 20, ΔR > 0.4) are looser than the partition threshold only for N_pγ ≥ 2 → residual migration is measured in the test jobs (N_pγ histograms in `work/p03/delphes_validation.json`).
2. **EW vs QCD**: by coupling order, verified by diagram counts (01 §3.1); interference is a signed separate term.
3. **Signal vs tHq/tt̄H**: 4F jets in all W±Hjj samples remove the resonant-top region (01 §3.1). tHW vs tt̄H and tWγ vs tt̄γ: diagram removal (`$$`), the ZH convention; DS as a systematic in Phase 06.
4. **W→ℓν vs W→τν**: by W decay flavour at the ME vertex; τ→ℓ events from the τ sample are background.
5. **Real vs fake/non-prompt leptons**: real non-prompt leptons (b/c decays) are simulated by Delphes directly ("direct" path, genuine cutflow). The parametrised j→ℓ path adds a pseudo-lepton (p_T = 0.6 p_T^j > 15 GeV) to the event's reco leptons, so any event that already has a reco lepton (p_T > 10) fails the second-lepton veto. Direct and parametrised paths are therefore disjoint by construction and are reported separately, never summed blindly. Removing Delphes non-prompt leptons *before* the parametrised path (so that the parametrised rate replaces rather than complements Delphes) is the stricter alternative, to be decided with D-WH-2.
6. **e→γ**: Delphes has no pixel-seed veto, and its `UniqueObjectFinder` gives photons priority (ZH 03 §4). In the parametrised e→γ treatment, reco photons within ΔR < 0.1 of a prompt gen electron are removed first and f_e→γ is applied to reco electrons.
7. **Frozen-ZH reuse vs new WH samples**: frozen ZH tt̄γ/tt̄H (dileptonic) are NOT reused: the WH top samples include dileptonic decays (≥ 1 ℓ, incl. τ), so reusing them would double count.
8. **H-pole vs γγ continuum interference**: absent from tree samples (no HAA vertex); quoted as a caveat (O(1 %) rate, O(100 MeV) mass shift, ggF literature), as in ZH.

**Caveat (self-review S1-2, severity 1): every fake pilot above is a LOWER bound, and the fake-sample design must change before production.** All fake samples are fixed-order with exactly two ME partons (Wγjj, W+jj, γγjj, VBF/ggF Hjj). When an ME parton is promoted to a fake γ/ℓ, at least one VBS tag jet must come from the parton shower, and the generator m_jj > 400 cut acts on the partons that become the fakes. Production samples for fake classes therefore need ≥ 1 (≥ 2) extra ME partons for single (double) fakes, e.g. Wγ + 3j and W + 4j (MLM-merged or fixed-order with the m_jj cut on any pair or removed), together with a twin test of any generator cut on the fake topology. ZH flagged the same limitation for its fake pilots (ZH 03 M6b).

All "not negligible" classes are continua constrained by the m_γγ sidebands. Their peak-window share is ≈ 10/55 of the fit range for a flat shape.

## 9. Decisions raised (also in 03 and STATUS)
- **D-WH-2 (N_MC rule for parametrised double-fake samples):** F_Wjj and T_tt_1l need 7.5·10⁸ / 8.0·10⁸ events under SPEC_G0 §1.5, which is infeasible. Proposal: extend the pre-declared HH effective-N policy (SPEC_G0 §4.3: truth-tag weighting, N_eff ≥ 10 in the final SR or a direct upper bound with the conservative variant in the robustness table) to WH fake-weighted samples (F_Wjj, T_tt_1l, and the fake-ℓ samples VBF/ggF H, QCD γγjj, if their N_req is not met). Needs human approval; it relaxes a §1 policy.
- **D-WH-3 (frozen ZH samples below N_req for the lost-lepton reuse):** ggZH (0.40), ZH→ττ (0.10), EW Zγγjj splits (0.40 each). Options: (a) new WH-run seeds extending these samples in canonical production (multi_r1 scope); (b) accept with an MC-stat nuisance, flagged in the robustness table. Recommendation: (a) for Zγγjj EW, (b) for ggZH/ZH→ττ if their WH-SR contribution is < 5 % of the background.
- **D-WH-4 (object-efficiency treatment):** the stock Delphes card gives electron efficiency 0.63 (barrel) / 0.50 (endcap) for prompt W-decay electrons (muons 0.83), reproducing the ZH deficit (ZH D11). WH has one electron in half the events. Recommendation: use the same CMS-like object-efficiency treatment as the ZH final analysis (`lib/scripts/analysis/final_analysis.py`) for consistency in the VH combination, with stock Delphes as a robustness variant.
