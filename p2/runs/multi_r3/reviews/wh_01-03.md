The cutflow fractions are unaffected. Only the absolute normalisation is wrong.
2. **D-WH-5 ("off-shell/on-shell = 0.89, unexplained") is a generator-cut artefact.** The off-shell card sets only `ptl 0` (cards/smoke/ew_lvhjj_offshell.mg5:32). The defaults `etal = 2.5` and `drjl = 0.4` stay active (work/smoke/ew_lvhjj_offshell/run_01_tag_1_banner.txt:270, :278). The on-shell chain has `cut_decays False`, so no lepton cuts. Applying |η_l|<2.5 and ΔR(j,l)>0.4 to the 2000-event S_EW LHE (work/test/S_EW_WHjj/S_EW_WHjj_s2301001.lhe.gz) leaves 0.892 ± 0.007, which matches the 0.89 ratio exactly. The proposed "≥11% pole-approximation syst" (01_signal_definition.md:24, STATUS.md:9) and the statement that the "nonresonant correction is NOT positive" are therefore unsupported. The fix is to rerun with `etal -1`, `drjl 0`, then recompare.
