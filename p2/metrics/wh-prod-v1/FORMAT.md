# FORMAT — WH production package (wh-prod-v1): physics conventions and data only

Process: pp → WHjj (EW-induced) with W → ℓν (ℓ = e, μ) and H → γγ, √s = 13.6 TeV, nominal luminosity L = 500 fb⁻¹ (N-rule luminosity 3000 fb⁻¹). This file contains conventions and input data, no analysis recipe, no selection and no code.

## 1. Layout
`<sample>/<sample>_s<seed>.root` — slim Delphes tree `Delphes` (CMS-style slim Delphes card `delphes_card_CMS_slim_v1`), one file per condor chunk (seed = MG5 seed of the chunk; unique over the package; the Pythia8 seed is 10⁸ + MG5 seed).
`<sample>/<sample>_s<seed>_gen.root` — truth **RNTuple** `GenTruth` (a ROOT RNTuple, not a TTree; read with an RNTuple reader, e.g. uproot ≥ 5.6 or ROOT's RNTupleReader), same entry order as `Delphes`.
`SAMPLES.csv` — one row per sample (σ, k, N_generated, N_Delphes, Σ weights, seeds, files, roles); `SHA256SUMS` — sha256 of every ROOT file.
Pinned stack: LCG_110 (x86_64-el9-gcc13-opt) with MG5_aMC v3.6.3 (upstream), **Pythia 8.317** (CP5 tune), **Delphes 3.5.1**, NNPDF3.1 NNLO (LHAPDF id 303600), seeds per chunk in the file names. Detector: Delphes CMS-like slim card `delphes_card_CMS_slim_v1` (ECal barrel resolution (1+0.64η²)·√(0.008²E² + 0.11²E + 0.40²) GeV, log-normal tower smearing, tower cuts EnergyMin 0.5 GeV / EnergySignificanceMin 2.0 after smearing), no pile-up.
**Package versions:** v1 contains the samples listed in SAMPLES.csv with N_generated > 0. v1.1 will add rows (`H_ggF_jj`; possibly others pending a human decision); SHA256SUMS will be regenerated then.

## 2. `Delphes` tree: collections (leaf names `<Collection>.<Leaf>`, units GeV)
`Jet` (anti-kT R = 0.4: PT, Eta, Phi, Mass, BTag, Charge, NCharged, NNeutrals, EhadOverEem, …), `GenJet` (same leaves, generator-level), `Photon` (PT, Eta, Phi, E, IsolationVar, IsolationVarRhoCorr, SumPt…, EhadOverEem, Particles; **the `Particle` branch (full generator record) is absent from these slim files, so the `Photon.Particles` references cannot be resolved here**; the generator information kept is the `GenTruth` tree and `GenJet`), `Electron` and `Muon` (PT, Eta, Phi, Charge, IsolationVar, IsolationVarRhoCorr, SumPt…, D0, DZ), `MissingET` (MET, Phi), `GenMissingET`, `ScalarHT` (HT), `Event` (Number, ProcessID, Weight, CrossSection(unused), Scale, AlphaQCD, ID1, ID2, X1, X2, PDF…), `Weight`, `EventLHEF`, `WeightLHEF`. Photon candidates are removed from the `Jet` collection (no reco Photon lies within ΔR < 0.2 of a reco Jet; a GenJet does contain the photon energy).
## 3. `GenTruth` RNTuple
`n_prompt_photons` — number of prompt generator photons with pT > 15 GeV, |η| < 2.5, ΔR > 0.4 to prompt leptons (pT > 10), to other such photons and to GenJets (pT > 20) not dominated by the photon itself; `gph_pt/eta/phi/prompt` — status-1 photons with pT > 10 GeV, |η| < 3 and a prompt flag (prompt = no hadron in the mother chain; quark/lepton/boson parents count as prompt); `glep_pt/eta/phi/pid/prompt` — status-1 e/μ with pT > 5 GeV and a prompt flag; `gH_*`, `gW_*`, `gZ_*`, `gt_*` — last-copy H, W, Z, top (pt, eta, phi, m).

## 4. Weights and normalisation (conventions)
`Event.Weight[0]` is the generator weight in **pb, signed**. It is the integrated cross-section estimate of the producing chunk (one condor job): within a sample its modulus varies from chunk to chunk by a few × 10⁻³ (e.g. F_Wjjjj chunks 411.8-415.0 pb against the sample σ of 414.7 pb) and is not exactly the sample σ listed in SAMPLES.csv (`sigma_pb_pre_decay` = LO MG5 gridpack-initialisation σ, pre-decay). For each sample SAMPLES.csv gives Σ_i Event.Weight_i over the delivered events and the ratio Σw/(N_generated·σ): it is 1.000 ± 0.006 for most samples; for the matched/accepted rows `H_QCD_WH_mlm` and `H_ZH_tautau_wh` (N_Delphes < N_generated by the MLM-matching / decay acceptance) |Event.Weight| is NOT σ (≈ 0.88 pb and 0.076 pb per accepted event against σ = 0.302 pb and 0.0253 pb) and the ratio is Σw/(N_generated·σ) = 0.981 (0.985 for `H_ttH_dilep_wh`, whose σ is from a test job). Events with negative weight are present (fraction 0-0.5 % for most samples, 1.4 % for the CP-odd coupling points; counted in SAMPLES.csv). The cross-section times external branching ratio, for any set S of events, is
 σ_BR(S) [fb] = 10³ × k × Σ_{i∈S} Event.Weight_i[pb] / N_generated,
and the expected number of events at luminosity L is L[fb⁻¹] × σ_BR(S); `N_generated` is the number of LHE events generated (SAMPLES.csv `N_generated_LHE`; **not** the number of Delphes entries for the two matched/accepted rows) and `k` is the product of the external branching-ratio factors listed per sample in SAMPLES.csv. Definitions: `BGG` = BR(H→γγ) = 0.00227 (LHCHWG YR4, mH = 125 GeV; H→γγ is forced in Pythia8 with 100 % conditional BR, so the generated σ is the production σ); `WF` = BR_PDG(W→eν, μν)/BR_MG5-tree(W→eν, μν) = 0.96008 (the W→e/μ ν decay is in the matrix element with the MG5 tree-level branching); `WFTAU` = BR_PDG(W→τν)/BR_MG5-tree(W→τν) (W→τν in the ME, τ decays in Pythia8); `WHAD` = BR_PDG(W→hadrons)/BR_MG5-tree; `ZF` = 0.9783 for Z→ee, μμ in the ME. The coupling samples carry H→γγ with the SM BR in `k`; the κ-dependence of BR(H→γγ) and of the total width is not included in the samples. The files contain no fake-factor or non-prompt weights.
**Further weights.** `WeightLHEF.Weight` (145 entries; 147 for `H_QCD_WH_mlm` and `H_ZH_tautau_wh`) are the LHE reweighting weights in pb, normalised like `Event.Weight` (same sign convention); entry j (0-based) is the LHE `<wgt id=j+1>` of the run (ids below are the 1-based LHE ids): ids 1-44 scale group (MUR, MUF) ∈ {0.5, 1, 2}² × dynamical-scale option, in the order (0.5,0.5), (0.5,1), (0.5,2), (1,0.5), (1,1), (1,2), (2,0.5), (2,1), (2,2) with, per pair, the entries "no dyn-scale change", `dyn_scale_choice` 1 (sum pT), 2 (HT), 3 (HT/2), 4 (√ŝ) — except (1,1), which has only options 1-4; **the entry with 0-based index 22 (= `<wgt id=23>`, (MUR, MUF) = (1, 1), `dyn_scale_choice` 3) equals `Event.Weight`** (checked on the first 2 000 events of the first S_EW_WHjj file; entry j (0-based) = `<wgt id=j+1>`); ids 45-145 PDF set NNPDF31_nnlo_as_0118 (LHAPDF 303600), id 45 = member 0 and ids 46-145 = replicas 303601-303700 (average over replicas, 100 replicas). In the two matched rows (147 entries) ids 45-46 are the emission-scale variations `alpsfact` = 0.5 and 2.0 (group "Emission scale variation", MUR = MUF = 1) and ids 47-147 are the PDF members 303600-303700. `Weight.Weight` (146 entries) = [`Event.Weight`, `WeightLHEF.Weight[0..144]`] (checked identical).

## 5. Samples and roles
Roles are in SAMPLES.csv. Signal `S_EW_WHjj` (W± H jj, QCD = 0, QED = 4, light-flavour initial/final jets, W→e/μ ν in ME) and the feed-down `S_EW_WHjj_taunu` (W→τν→ℓνν); irreducible `B_QCD_Waajj_nomjj`, `B_EW_Waajj_nomjj` (Wγγjj, no generator m_jj cut); prompt-photon + fake rows `F_EW_Wajj_nomjj` (EW Wγjj), `F_QCD_Wajjj_nomjj` (QCD Wγ + 3 jets: matrix element with exactly three outgoing partons each with pT > 20 GeV, `p p > w± a j j j QCD=3 QED=2`; no 2-jet + parton-shower component and no MLM merging in this sample; the 2-jet row `F_QCD_Wajj_nomjj` was withdrawn and is not in the package), two-fake rows `F_Wjjjj` (W + 4 jets) and `T_ttjj_1l` (tt̄jj, one lepton), `T_tta_1l`, `T_tWa_1l`, `T_ttaa_1l` (top + photons), V+γ rows `V_WWa_WH`, `V_WZa_WH`, `V_ZZa_WH` (no ME jets: jets from the parton shower only, lost-lepton / e→γ), Higgs rows `H_QCD_WH_mlm` (QCD WH + MLM-matched jets), `H_ggZH_wh`, `H_ZH_tautau_wh`, `H_ttH_1l`, `H_ttH_dilep_wh`, `H_tHq_lep`, `H_tHW_1l`, `H_VBFH_jj` (VBF H + hadronic VH, EW order 3), non-prompt-lepton rows `Q_aajj` (γγ + 2 jets, no lepton in the ME) and `Q_aajjj` (γγ + 3 jets: matrix element `p p > a a j j j` with exactly three outgoing partons each with pT > 20 GeV; no 2-jet + parton-shower component, no merging); `H_QCD_WHjj_fixed` is a cross-check only (not in the signal or background). `H_ggF_jj` (loop-induced ggF-like H + 2 jets) is a later row (see SAMPLES.csv). The frozen ZH-study samples used as W-analysis backgrounds (lost-lepton Zγγjj, ZHjj, Zγjj with e→γ) are listed in SAMPLES.csv as READ-ONLY rows pointing to the frozen ZH production (path, σ, k, available N); their files are not copied or modified. **Coupling-study samples** `K_EW_WHjj_*` (model HC_NLO_X0_kVV: official Higgs-characterisation UFO with two extra parameters kSMW and kSMZ; κ_W = kSM·kSMW, κ_Z = kSM·kSMZ, λ_WZ = κ_W/κ_Z; effective X0γγ/Zγ/gg and derivative couplings set to 0): λ_WZ ∈ {−1, −0.5, 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5} at κ_Z = 1 (`lam_m100 … lam_p150`), (κ_W, κ_Z) = (1.5, 0.75) (`kap_W15_Z075`) and (0.5, 1.5) (`kap_W05_Z15`), and two CP-mixed points (cos α = 1/√2, kSM = √2, kAzz = kAww = ±2√2, `cpo_p2`, `cpo_m2`); same generator cuts as the SM signal. σ(κ_W, κ_Z) is an exact quadratic form, σ = α κ_W² + β κ_W κ_Z + γ κ_Z². Samples not produced as separate files: the 2-jet `F_QCD_Wajj_nomjj`, `I_WHjj` (interference, signed σ only).
Generator settings per sample are in SAMPLES.csv (`generator_cuts`): ptj > 20, |ηj| < 5, ΔRjj > 0.4 and ΔRjℓ > 0.4 for the EW signal-like rows, pγ_T > 25 GeV (15 GeV for T_tta/T_tWa/V_*), |ηγ| < 2.5; no cut on decay leptons (cut_decays False); dynamical scale choice 3; QED/QCD orders as in the `process` column.

### 6c. Non-prompt leptons (jet → ℓ), rows `Q_aajj`, `H_VBFH_jj`, `H_ggF_jj`
Per reco jet (pT > 25 GeV, |η| < 2.5) probability f_ℓ to be reconstructed as an isolated lepton of either flavour, lepton pT = x·pT(jet) with x ~ N(0.85, 0.1) truncated to [0.5, 1] (an **uncited assumption** used in the pilots, not a measurement), lepton pT > 25 GeV. **Range (D28): f_ℓ ∈ {2×10⁻⁵ (tight), 2×10⁻⁴ (medium, nominal), 2×10⁻³ (loose)}**, from the per-jet electron-identification rates 1/(jet rejection) of ATLAS, Eur. Phys. J. C 72 (2012) 1909, arXiv:1110.3174, p. 4, Sec. 4.4 (loose/medium/tight expected jet rejection ≈ 500 / 5 000 / 50 000, MC dijet, identification only, no isolation). Caveats: arXiv:1902.04655 (Eur. Phys. J. C 79 (2019) 639): absolute rejections carry large uncertainties from the definition of the denominator; muons and heavy-flavour leptons are not covered by the cited electron numbers; related per-hadron number (not per jet): CMS charged-hadron → muon misidentification 0.1-0.5 % per hadron track, arXiv:1804.04528 (JINST 13 (2018) P06015), p. 20. The range is the D28 bracket over the cited identification rates. The direct Delphes non-prompt-lepton component (real reco leptons inside jets) is kept in the files.

D28: f_ℓ bracket of 6c. D29: an ME-jet sample only where the parton-shower-modelled yield exceeds 10 % of the SR background, otherwise a 100 % PS-modelling uncertainty.
