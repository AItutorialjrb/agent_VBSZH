## Definition
Signal = full coherent tree amplitude at orders (QCD,QED)=(0,4) for pp -> W+- H j j (5F proton/jets g,u,c,d,s,b), W decayed in the MG5 ME chain (W+->l+ nu, W-->l- nu~, l=e,mu; spin correlated), H left undecayed in the LHE and forced to gamma gamma in Pythia8 (`25:onMode off, 25:onIfMatch 22 22`; conditional BR=1). SM H->gamma gamma is loop induced: no tree `h > a a` is used. B(H->gg)=0.002270 (YR4, mH=125) applied once externally; W branching handled by WF (below).
Syntax validated by process/diagram output: `generate p p > w+ h j j QCD=0 QED=4` -> 52 processes / 896 diagrams (identical for W-); QCD `QCD=2 QED=2` -> 60 / 200; interference `QCD^2==2 QED^2==6` -> 52 / 1032 (subset of EW+QCD, as expected); ME-chain with both charges (gridpack card) 2 proc / 1796 (EW), 404 (QCD). Logs `work/smoke/*/mg5.log`.
**Gauge invariance / labels.** All diagrams at (0,4) are kept coherently: VBF-like W/Z exchange, VBS, Higgsstrahlung-like s-channel V* -> V H with V->jj, WWH/WZH tribosons with hadronic V, quark-line radiation. "VBF-like" / "VBS-like" is a later fiducial region (mjj, Delta eta), not a diagram subset. Diagnostic only (gauge-dependent, not a signal definition): vetoing s-channel W/Z propagators (`$ w+ w- z`) leaves 75.12 +- 0.71 of 83.99 +- 0.82 fb (89.4%) for W+, i.e. ~10.6% of the rate sits in diagrams with resonant s-channel V. The EW signal therefore contains a hadronic-V (VVH) component that the later m_jj/d eta selection (not diagram labels) must treat; it is part of the signal definition.
